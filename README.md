# Academic AI Workflow

A university project exploring how cloud-based AI tools can support and improve the academic research and publication-preparation workflow.

## Project overview

The project examines a practical workflow for finding academic literature, organizing sources, preparing a literature review, checking bibliographic references, and creating a presentation. The goal is to use AI as an assistant while keeping source verification and academic judgment under human control.

**Project title:** Improving the Efficiency of Academic Publication Preparation with Cloud-Based AI Tools

## Objectives

- Explore how AI-assisted tools can support academic research and writing.
- Organize selected academic sources in a reference-management workflow.
- Prepare a structured literature analysis.
- Verify reference metadata against bibliographic records, including DOI information.
- Present the workflow, its benefits, and its limitations.

## Tools and resources

- **Zotero** — collecting and organizing references.
- **Cloud-based AI assistance** — supporting research organization, drafting, and review.
- **Crossref** — checking bibliographic metadata and DOI records.
- **Google Drive** — organizing and sharing project materials.
- **Microsoft Word and PowerPoint** — preparing the written deliverables and presentation.

Only tools and steps actually used in the project are described here. Other academic-writing products were considered but are not presented as tested tools.

## Research workflow

1. Define the research topic and the scope of the project.
2. Search for relevant academic publications.
3. Collect and organize the selected references in Zotero.
4. Review the available article text and abstracts.
5. Prepare the literature analysis with clear distinctions between full-text and abstract-based evidence.
6. Check reference details such as title, authors, publication year, journal information, and DOI.
7. Prepare the final report and presentation.

## Citation verification

Bibliographic details for the selected references were checked against Crossref records. A matching DOI record supports the accuracy of the metadata, but it does not by itself validate an article's findings or replace reading the source.

Some selected publications were available only through their abstracts or linked records. Conclusions based on those sources should therefore be interpreted with that limitation in mind.

## Results and limitations

The project produced an organized reference collection, a literature-analysis document, a citation-audit document, and a presentation describing the workflow.

This is a practical workflow demonstration, not a controlled experiment. No measured time-saving percentage or causal improvement is claimed. The quality of AI-assisted output still depends on source quality, careful prompting, and human verification.

## Academic integrity and responsible use

AI-generated or AI-assisted text should be reviewed before academic submission. Always consult the original sources, verify citations, follow your institution's rules on AI use, and disclose assistance when required.
